# Client Discovery & Intake Questionnaire — tapp

To configure the brand identity, starter catalogue, and visual tone for **tapp**, please provide details for the following 5 discovery items:

1. **Brand Identity & Assets**:
   - What is the exact brand name capitalization and tagline?
   - Please provide a high-resolution logo (SVG or transparent PNG) and brand favicon.
   - Do you have specific primary and secondary brand hex colors (e.g., `#0F172A`, `#3B82F6`)?

2. **Starter Product Catalog (3 to 5 Official Products)**:
   - Provide titles, categories (e.g., CleanTech, SaaS, AI, Hardware), taglines, descriptions, and high-quality product images for 3–5 initial featured products.

3. **Inspiration & Benchmark Sites**:
   - Which showcase/directory websites represent your target look and feel? (e.g., Product Hunt, Bento.me, Linear, Indie Hackers).

4. **Product Submission Form Fields**:
   - Are there any custom fields needed during product submission besides Name, Category, Tagline, Description, and Image? (e.g., Live Website URL, Demo Video link, Pricing model).

5. **Admin Access Account**:
   - What email address will the founder/client use to sign in for administrative moderation privileges?
